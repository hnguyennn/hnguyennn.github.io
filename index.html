During this past summer, I participated in the Computational and Applied Math REU at UCLA in the Quantum Sensing group, where I was supervised by Professor Andrea Bertozzi and mentored by PhD Candidate Matthew Tyler. I combined two algorithms, GRSU (Graph-Regularized Semisupervised Unmixing) and Augmented Linear Mixing Model (ALMM) to create Graph-ALMM for Hyperspectral and Chemical Unmixing problems.

Our abstract has been accepted to SPIE Photonics West for an Oral Presentation and JMM for a Poster Presentation! I will be presenting at both. You can view our abstracts below.
